# Support

## Frequent questions

**The app says "No connection to the Shelly cloud".**
Your Mac cannot reach Shelly right now. The figures on screen are the last ones
read, and the app tells you when they are from. It does not mean your power is
out: the app never reports an outage when it simply could not ask.

**I get no notifications.**
Check *Settings → Notifications → Send a test notification*. If nothing appears,
open the macOS notification settings from the same panel and make sure Vatio is
allowed and its style is Banners or Alerts.

**An alert fired once and never again.**
Alerts fire when a reading crosses their limit and stay quiet until it comes
back to the other side. If a reading sits permanently beyond the limit, lower or
raise the threshold so it only triggers when something actually happens.

**The cost does not match my bill.**
Copy the tariff from your bill in *Settings → Tariff*: price or tiers, currency,
fixed charge and any surcharge. Vatio estimates with the numbers you enter.
